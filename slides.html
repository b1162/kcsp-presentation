OUR KILLER FEATURE
ZERO LIABILITY

The Zero-Credential Engine:
Realism without the liability.

Traditional simulation tools create massive legal headaches: what if an employee inputs their real corporate SSO password into your simulation database? KCSP solves this permanently at the architectural level.

✓ Client-Side Interception: The browser DOM hook discards passwords in memory before the request is even dispatched.
✓ Binary Telemetry Only: Backend records strictly INTERACTED = TRUE. Zero plaintext secrets on wire or disk.
✓ Legal & Compliance Nirvana: Fully aligned with union labor laws, GDPR, and ISO 27001 ethics rules.
