Core Ethical Safety Guarantee

Zero-Credential Safe Simulation Engine

LEGAL & ETHICAL SHIELD

Eliminate Credential Liabilities Without Sacrificing Simulation Realism

In conventional simulation tools, employees accidentally submitting real corporate credentials risk exposing secrets to internal admins and third-party SaaS vendors.

Client-Side Form Interception Forms on simulation pages are intercepted via DOM JavaScript. Plaintext passwords, pins, and tokens are scrubbed in client memory before network transmission.

Zero Credential Storage The backend API (/api/simulation/interact) records strictly a binary event: User Interacted = True. No secrets are ever persisted.

Full Compliance Protection Satisfies union agreements, GDPR Article 5 data minimization, and ISO 27001 ethics guidelines.
